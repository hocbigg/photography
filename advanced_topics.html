This page branches the Core Curriculum into five independent specializations. Choose one or a few tracks aligned with your specific interests and goals rather than attempting to complete every track sequentially.

  • Documentary & Photojournalism - The craft, field ethics, and reporting methods required to document real people, conflict, and social issues for publication.
  • Studio & Portrait Lighting - Complete command of artificial illumination, from optical physics and light shaping to multi-point studio configurations and editorial portraiture.
  • Post-Production, Color & Print - Advanced raw sensor data processing, strict color management, non-destructive retouching, and exhibition-grade fine art printing.
  • Fine Art & Contemporary Practice - Developing sustained thematic bodies of work, photobook sequencing, creative problem-solving, and engaging with the contemporary art ecosystem.
  • Photographic Theory & Criticism - The advanced philosophical and semiotic literature investigating visual culture, the apparatus, and the socio-political power of the lens.

Documentary & Photojournalism

This track covers the ethical standards, narrative structures, and practical field methodologies necessary to execute responsible long-form visual journalism.

Ethics and professional standards: Code of Ethics for Visual Journalists (National Press Photographers Association) - The industry-standard ethical framework establishing legal, manipulation, and subject-treatment boundaries for visual reporters.

Trauma-informed visual reporting: Dart Center Style Guide for Trauma-Informed Journalism (Dart Center for Journalism & Trauma) - A practical, evidence-based guide for ethically documenting victims of disaster, violence, and social vulnerability without causing secondary harm.

Visual reporting and photo essays: Photojournalism: The Professionals' Approach, 7th Edition (Kenneth Kobre) - The canonical professional textbook covering beat reporting, pitch development, caption writing, and narrative picture editing.

Contemporary documentary practice: Everyday Projects Education: The Essentials (The Everyday Projects via PhotoWings) - A free video masterclass series taught by working photojournalists focused on non-stereotyping, community-engaged documentary storytelling.

Studio & Portrait Lighting

This track covers the deliberate manipulation of artificial light, ranging from geometric optical behavior to complex multi-light commercial studio configurations.

Physics of studio illumination: Laws of Light Tutorial Series (Jay P. Morgan, The Slanted Lens) - A structured video course analyzing highlight, core shadow, reflected fill, and falloff on geometric forms to predict how light interacts with any subject.

Systematic studio lighting setups: Master Lighting Guide for Portrait Photographers (Christopher Grey) - A comprehensive studio manual detailing key, fill, hair, and background light ratios, modifier selections, and standardized multi-strobe patterns.

Editorial portrait lighting: Gregory Heisler: 50 Portraits (Gregory Heisler) - A masterclass in conceptual portraiture that deconstructs fifty complex editorial setups alongside the artistic and technical decisions behind them.

Post-Production, Color & Print

This track covers the technical execution of image finishing, including sensor data interpretation, color space standardization, frequency separation, and tangible print output.

Advanced RAW processing: The Digital Negative: Raw Image Processing in Lightroom, Camera Raw, and Photoshop, 2nd Edition (Jeff Schewe) - An authoritative guide to raw demosaicing, sensor dynamic range recovery, parametric tone adjustments, and capture sharpening.

Color management and calibration: Colour Management Information and Training (Keith Cooper, Northlight Images) - A rigorous, free technical reference covering hardware monitor calibration, ICC profile generation, rendering intents, and lighting-booth evaluation.

Advanced retouching and masking: Photoshop Retouching Course (Unmesh Dinda, PiXimperfect) - search 'Photoshop Retouching Course PiXimperfect' on YouTube - A structured tutorial sequence teaching frequency separation, luminosity masking, dodge-and-burn mechanics, and seamless texture preservation.

Fine art digital printing: The Digital Print: Preparing Images in Lightroom and Photoshop for Printing (Jeff Schewe) - The definitive text on exhibition output, soft-proofing, printer color gamuts, paper coatings, and output resolution scaling.

Fine Art & Contemporary Practice

This track explores the conceptual frameworks, project generation techniques, sequencing methodologies, and professional dialogues that define contemporary artistic photography.

Themes in contemporary photography: The Photograph as Contemporary Art, updated edition (Charlotte Cotton) - The standard survey categorizing the conceptual strategies, aesthetics, and themes dominating gallery and museum photography since the 1980s.

Project ideation and constraints: The Photographer's Playbook (Jason Fulford and Gregory Halpern, Aperture) - A collection of over 300 assignments, prompts, and philosophical exercises designed to break creative stagnation and build coherent photographic series.

Photobook history and sequencing: The Photobook: A History, Volume I (Martin Parr and Gerry Badger) - The seminal study on the photobook as the primary medium of artistic photographic expression, emphasizing narrative sequencing and layout pacing.

Contemporary artist dialogues: i3: Images, Ideas, Inspiration Lecture Series (School of Visual Arts) - search 'SVA i3 Images Ideas Inspiration' on YouTube - An extensive archive of hour-long guest lectures by leading fine art photographers discussing working methods, project development, and exhibition curation.

Photographic Theory & Criticism

This track investigates the intellectual, cultural, and political stakes of the medium through its most influential philosophical and critical texts.

Ideology and the visual image: Ways of Seeing, BBC Television Series (John Berger) - A seminal four-part broadcast analyzing the politics of looking, the commodification of art, and the ideological conditioning embedded in visual media.

Phenomenology of the photograph: Camera Lucida: Reflections on Photography (Roland Barthes) - The foundational philosophical essay establishing the concepts of studium and punctum to explore memory, grief, and the ontological reality of captured time.

Philosophy of the apparatus: Towards a Philosophy of Photography (Vilém Flusser) - A concise media-theory treatise arguing that automated cameras program human behavior and examining how photographers can create meaning against the program.

Photography and civic power: The Civil Contract of Photography (Ariella Azoulay) - An essential modern text exploring photography as an ongoing social contract among camera, subject, and spectator that transcends state sovereignty.
